## Candidate Heuristics
- After a family has a clear teaching scaffold, keep the first diagnostic batch to one lever.
- Convert official hints into a narrow follow-up batch; "shorter window", alternate input, or alternate neutralization should be tested one at a time.
- The official example pages are reusable because they separate hypothesis, implementation, hint, and simulation settings.
- When a family is still exploratory and lacks real submission evidence, treat the example page as a design reference only.
- The beginner operating_income example supports the current one-field history-rank family; the bronze examples support future ratio/cash-flow and volatility branches.
- The operating_income ridge-sweep from batch 08 shows the same principle in practice: one fixed history rank plus a narrow smoothing sweep is better than broad operator expansion.
- The capital-structure batch repeats the same principle in a different family: the 63d leverage anchor stays better than the 21d smoothing clone, so branch structure matters more than another same-family tweak.
- The capital-structure batch 02 adds a narrower version of the same lesson: the subindustry probe improves the test-period readout, but it still does not surface real Check Submission evidence or a non-null subuniverse verdict, so a structural branch should be followed by a distinct ratio family rather than more same-lane smoothing.
- The external literature adds two durable heuristics: low correlation usually comes from a genuinely new information source or group / neutralization change, and robustness requires checking small parameter perturbations plus universe / region shifts.
