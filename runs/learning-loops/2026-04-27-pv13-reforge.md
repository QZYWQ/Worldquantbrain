# pv13 Structural Reforge Learning Loop

## Why this batch ran
RECURVE round 2 showed the pv13 relationship-data lane was missing the legacy winning pattern of peer context plus state construction. The lead anchor was still good on Fitness, but it kept getting stopped by `LOW_SHARPE`, so this batch tested whether the missing structural layers could close the gap without leaving pv13.

## What was tested
- P0: `group_rank(ts_rank(pv13_ustomergraphrank_page_rank, 150), industry)`
- P1: `group_rank(ts_rank(ts_mean(pv13_ustomergraphrank_page_rank, 63), 252), industry)`
- P2: `ts_rank(group_rank(pv13_ustomergraphrank_page_rank / pv13_com_page_rank, industry), 90)`

## What we learned
- Peer context helped efficiency more than edge: P0 and P1 lowered turnover, but neither beat the anchor on TEST Sharpe.
- The slower state-construction layer in P1 was the best of the three, which supports the RECURVE hypothesis, but the lift was still too small to matter.
- The ratio hybrid gave up too much Sharpe and looked less robust on the sub-universe check.

## Practical takeaway
The pv13 customer-centrality family is structurally plausible, but stacking more peer-relative shape onto the same lane does not solve the official `LOW_SHARPE` gate. If we revisit pv13, the next attempt should change the family identity or field mix more meaningfully instead of polishing this anchor again.
