# price / range / volume alpha. Settings: USA TOP3000 D1, platform
# neutralization MARKET, platform decay 3, testPeriod P1Y.

# socialmedia12 update gate: keep the strong technical score but only refresh
# when smoothed fast social sentiment is above the cross-sectional median.
