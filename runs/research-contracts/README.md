# Research Contracts

Store durable family research-contract gate outputs here when they should remain part of the project history.

These artifacts sit in front of local mining and answer a narrower question than alpha quality:

- did the family explicitly declare its mechanism, settings, and kill condition
- do universe, delay, holding frequency, and neutralization choices agree with the family metadata and expressions
- is the NaN / pasteurization / unit-handling policy explicit enough to stop pseudo-branches before mining
