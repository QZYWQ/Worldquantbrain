To incorporate volatility into your Alpha for the Chinese market, consider constructing a proxy for volatility using available datasets. For example:

Price Range: Use the daily high-low price range or the difference between the daily open and close as a measure of volatility.

Rolling Standard Deviation: Compute the rolling standard deviation of daily returns over a fixed period (e.g., 5 or 10 days) to capture recent volatility trends.

Volume Implied Volatility: Analyze the relationship between changes in trading volume and price movements as an indirect indicator of volatility.

These methods can help you approximate volatility even when explicit datasets are unavailable.
